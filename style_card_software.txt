STYLE CARD v1.0 — Depth First (software/technical documentaries)
================================================================
Prepend this block to EVERY image-generation prompt for the Depth First channel.
This is NOT style_card.txt — that one is The Engineering Atlas (warm parchment,
golden hour, stone). The two channels are deliberately opposite palettes
(brand_guide_software.md §3). Using the wrong card produces off-brand plates and
you only find out after paying for them.

WIDESCREEN 16:9 LANDSCAPE, 1920x1080 — never square, never portrait. The frame is
a wide cinematic rectangle; compose across its full width.
Isometric schematic technical illustration, drafting-table discipline.
Near-black blue-black ground (#0B0E14) — never pure black, never warm.
Cold graphite structure and linework (#2A3240), surfaces in slate (#161B26).
Electric cyan (#22D3EE) as the SINGLE pop colour, carried only by glowing
windows, rim-light and illuminated cable runs.
Cold rim-light from the upper left. Night, or flat overcast — never daylight.
NO warm tan, NO ochre, NO parchment, NO golden hour, NO sunset tones.
Precise geometric lines, high architectural precision, deep moody dark background.
Disciplined like a drafting table, not a glossy product render.
No humans visible (or tiny unlit silhouettes for scale only).
No text, letters, numbers, watermarks or logos anywhere in the image.
The CANVAS is 16:9 edge to edge — no letterboxing, no borders, no square canvas
inside a wider one. This is about the canvas, NOT the content: do NOT add extra
objects, duplicates or product-lineup arrangements to fill the width. Large empty
dark space is deliberate and correct — this brand composes around negative space.
Render exactly the subject described, in the quantity described, and nothing else.

--- ACCURACY INVARIANTS (continuity_registry.accuracy — non-negotiable) ---
Cooling-tower plumes are CONDENSING WATER VAPOUR: bright white / pale blue,
soft-edged, billowing gently. A fission plant burns nothing, so there is NEVER
smoke, soot, exhaust, a grey or brown plume, storm cloud, or lightning.
Power flow is shown as STEADILY GLOWING conductors and cable runs — never as
lightning bolts, sparks or electrical arcing. Do not use the word "arcs".
Three Mile Island has TWO hyperbolic towers; the reactor itself is the squat
domed containment building, not the towers.

--- PER-SCENE ADD-ONS (appended automatically by prompt_builder) ---
Scene type recipe · Subject · visual_facts · accent clause · composition hint.

--- STYLE ANCHOR ---
Depth First has NO anchor library yet (it starts empty — EA's assets do not
transfer). Scene 01 is generated first and then passed as the reference image
for every subsequent scene, which is what locks the look across the video.
